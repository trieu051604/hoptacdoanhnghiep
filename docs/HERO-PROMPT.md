## Prompt đã dùng

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide photographic hero banner background for a Vietnamese university career website, landscape about 3:1 (1920 by 640 composition). Create one finished image asset, NOT a website screenshot or UI mockup. Photorealistic polished university recruitment advertising photo. Composition: the RIGHT 55 percent shows three cheerful Vietnamese university students aged 20-23, a young man in an orange polo and backpack at the left of the group, a young woman in an orange polo holding a silver laptop in the center, a young man with black glasses wearing a dark jacket with an orange shirt and backpack on the right. Waist-up framing, full heads, natural smiles looking slightly up toward their futures. Behind them a modern white university building, lush palms and green campus gardens, blue sky with clouds, warm bright daylight. Optional understated building sign 'FPT UNIVERSITY', no other words. The LEFT 45 percent is mostly clear ivory and pale peach with very subtle watercolor paper texture and soft organic orange/cream brush shapes, minimal contrast and spacious room for live HTML headline, description, search field and filter pills, all to be placed by code later. A translucent orange rounded geometric ribbon behind the group near center, tasteful, not obscuring faces. Smooth organic transition between left cream area and right campus photograph. Orange/white brand palette, realistic anatomy, realistic university students, premium art direction. No heading, no slogan, no baked-in UI, no buttons, no search field, no small text, no watermark. Keep important faces in the upper right and image bottom suitable for cropping to a 400-460 px tall banner. Wide panoramic layout, finished raster asset.
